DP-700 Stem Decoder

The last sentence of a stem names the constraint that decides between two answers that would both work. Read it first, then match the phrase below.

How to read a stem

  1. Read the ask and the constraint. "What should you use? The solution must minimize development effort." Now you know the scoring rule before you see the scenario.
  2. Scan the scenario for keywords. Circle the source, the target, the people, and any number: data volume, latency, a role, a SKU.
  3. Eliminate before you choose. Cross out options that break a stated requirement, then pick the one that best satisfies the constraint. Two options often both work; only one is the least effort, cost, or privilege.

Trap words and distractor patterns

Small words in the stem flip the answer; distractors are usually real features used in the wrong place.
